## 4. One bounded implementation milestone: the photograph returns

**Proposed scope, not implemented:** one evidence entry, **two transmission paths**, **two Sable decisions**, two off-screen outcomes and one later update family. Use only a shop, trial bar/private conversation space and home; the clinic stays off-screen. The listing authenticates the photo entry, not a second independent investigative route.

1. **Discover:** player inspects the print and its provenance listing, then compares them with Sable's earlier, explicitly heard account of continuous hospitalization on that date. Seeing Sable in a photograph alone is not enough to know what it contradicts. The journal separates observed content, dating basis, Sable's account and the player's inference.
2. **Disclose:** either show the accessible evidence to present Sable, or show it to Vesper and explicitly ask them to relay it. Vesper inspects the actual objects before scheduling a private remote call 24 hours later. A mere unsupported player assertion is stored as a claim and does not pass the credibility gate. SHOW retains custody; GIVE transfers it. The relay conveys Vesper's witnessed inspection, not an imaginary attachment or duplicate print.
3. **Sable decides:** record an immutable receipt and evaluate Sable's own evidence, distress, immediate work obligations, willingness and available independent appointment. With credible evidence, manageable distress and an available acceptable appointment, choose **Investigate Formally**. Otherwise, with credible evidence, choose **Document and Prepare**. Without it, record doubt and request corroboration; no medical event is scheduled. The latter is an incomplete lead, not a third full decision route. Trust can affect willingness but cannot replace evidence or be the sole switch.
4. **Agency:** support, respectful disagreement, pressure or leaving have authored responses; support/pressure can affect Sable's distress before the decision. Sable owns the choice. Both direct and relay routes can reach either outcome using the supported contextual replies. Refusing further help does not cancel Sable's independently accepted appointment. No compulsory payment, romance gate or player-commanded examination.
5. **Off-screen action:** formal investigation creates a limited report in Sable's custody after 72 hours and records the missed shift/return; documentation creates Sable's dated notebook after 24 hours, containing remembered observations and uncertainties rather than restored memories. Events execute while the player is elsewhere. Record the reasons actually used for the decision and the activity that actually completed; do not retroactively infer these at encounter time.
6. **Later encounter:** at the next staffed private bar opportunity, Sable's update reads the receipt, decision and outcome. It identifies the photograph/listing and direct showing or Vesper's call accurately. Current custody controls whether Sable can show the print; only a real report/notebook can be shown or transferred. Player replies may ask about the result, address the disclosure method, offer support, disagree or simply listen. The update is delivered once, and any follow-up remains consistent on return.

One opening-evening target is enough to make the trial accessible: discover the contradiction and obtain direct acknowledgement, or explicit confirmation that Vesper will relay it. The later payoff remains days later. Rest should explain the next time reached, process all intervening events and preserve evidence left in rooms. Returning at the wrong time produces honest availability and a reachable next opportunity, not a permanently lost update. Work preparation and ordinary conversation remain alongside the investigation; learning a contradiction does not erase Sable's life.
