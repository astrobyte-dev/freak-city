## 2. Safe stopping point and paused work

The graphical experiment stops at its completed one-room beat, with its code, dependency lockfile, tests and evidence retained under `prototype/`. Its former next steps—richer prototype evidence responses, another room, navigation/interaction anchors, further graphical interface work and a general structured-action API for expansion—are paused. Vestibule activation, additional room/character art, street/Velvet polish, animation and model experiments remain deferred. No graphical work is needed for the next milestone. No reset, discard, commit, push, merge or deployment forms part of this transition.

Retain the prototype's useful findings: a question must offer a real answer; visible subjects must match their targets; changing intent must cancel stale actions; the latest response must remain readable; private thoughts must be relevant and distinct from speech; custody and knowledge must come from the simulation. These are interaction requirements for the text game too.

## 4. Highest-value next playable milestone

**One coherent arrival: meet Inez, inspect the invitation, have an ordinary exchange with Mara, and leave with an actionable investigative lead and a remembered interaction.** This replaces a sequence of disconnected parser fixes as the delivery unit.

Player experience: take the envelope, use the named entrance, answer Inez's name request naturally or decline disclosure, inspect/read/show the invitation intentionally, and ask a follow-up that explains how to pursue the existing routing evidence without revealing the seeded sender. At the bar, answer the offered drink question and receive a characterful response. Optionally leave the closed envelope with Inez, return and collect it, receiving the existing appropriate callback. A brief private observation can add comedy or sincerity without becoming speech or NPC knowledge.

Scope: the existing taxi, street, vestibule and bar; only enough washroom reconciliation to make its already-exposed person/affordances honest. Reuse current transactions, authored choices, objects, schedules and agreement records. Make focused parser/context and prose edits, plus short authored replies for the opening's actual questions. No new rooms, general planner, relationship-system expansion, runtime AI, graphical UI or art production. The destination of the investigative lead must follow existing evidence access and timing, not a newly invented clue or shortcut.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Fresh descriptions match location, presence and custody before/after take, put, travel and reload. No invented umbrella, wristband, bulb transfer, player reply or absent actor is asserted.
2. Named visible doors and invitation/letter references work through existing exit/visibility/lock rules. Local descriptive people never identify an absent unrelated NPC; explicit names, remote contact and genuine ambiguity retain correct behavior. The washroom's anonymous presence receives a bounded authored resolution, not just a misleading error renamed.
3. Every question in this slice has meaningful natural replies, refusal or clarification. Name replies preserve chosen identity and remembered disclosure; drinks and sender follow-ups stay on topic. No guessed sender, inferred consent or accidental agreement/disclosure.
4. Repeat TALK, a brief interruption and save/reload preserve who was met and what was answered. Returning acknowledges relevant history without replaying introductions or jumping to unrelated scenes. Expired context clarifies rather than silently reinterpreting a reply.
5. The invitation sequence ends with a specific, reachable next step under current schedule/access rules. Optional safekeeping still supports acceptance/refusal, collection, missed time and repair with unchanged custody/provenance guarantees.
6. A fresh main-UI playthrough using consecutive typing can finish the beat without a hidden command list. Review alternate reply wording, an interruption/reload, a declined offer and late collection. Focused regressions and the required repository checks pass; human review judges whether dialogue, private humour and sincerity feel coherent. Passing parser tests alone is not opening acceptance.

Transition validation: nine synthetic runs, every returned state serialized and save-validated; **847 existing tests passed** across alias, commitments and natural-parser suites. Earlier broad/browser results remain historical. This handoff changes documentation and adds the reproducible review script/evidence only; no gameplay fixes or broad rewrite were implemented.
