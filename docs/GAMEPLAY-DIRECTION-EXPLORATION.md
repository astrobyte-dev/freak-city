# Gameplay direction: agreed experience, proposed mechanics

**2026-09-17 direction update:** active development returns to the original text-led game. The graphical prototype is retained as an inactive experiment; visual development is deferred, with static scene imagery, state-driven overlays and potentially minimal animation as the intended future presentation. The [transition handoff](TEXT-LED-TRANSITION.md) supersedes older continuation recommendations and identifies the next bounded opening milestone. The briefs and earlier implementation records below remain preserved. This handoff does not implement a broad rewrite or authorize graphical expansion.

Owner direction updated 2026-09-14. The initial discussion is complete. The supplied [gameplay brief](design/FREAK-CITY-gameplay-design-brief.md) and [player identity brief](design/FREAK-CITY-player-identity-design.md) are retained verbatim in the repository. Their statements about not yet being added to the repository describe their original delivery status.

**Agreed direction:** an ongoing adult neo-noir city across multiple nights, natural typed interaction, independent character goals, persistent consequences and substantial lives beyond the mystery. Attraction, jealousy, trust and strong character-specific fetish themes are integral to the intended experience, alongside ordinary care, work, humour and non-romantic companionship. Participation remains optional and core mystery access does not depend on romance.

**Decided player identity direction:** a lightly defined adult protagonist with the existing opening and seeded truths, alias, optional identity/profile and background choices, and intentions expressed through play. Explicit player facts, experienced history and individual NPC impressions are distinct. Content preferences do not disclose desires to NPCs or establish consent. The identity brief supplies the detailed decisions; these are not claims about current implementation.

**Proposed mechanics and staging:** commitment records, bounded adaptive intentions, richer physical and language rules, relationship dimensions, scene eligibility and later-night persistence still require concrete design and validation. The briefs do not approve a broad rewrite or every recommended mechanic. Existing lore, character identities and content boundaries remain authoritative. Inez remains non-romantic.

The owner reviewed the [assessment and proposed milestone](design/SOCIAL-SITUATION-ASSESSMENT-AND-PLAN.md) and authorized [one local Inez envelope-safekeeping implementation](design/INEZ-ENVELOPE-IMPLEMENTATION.md), refined around her real availability, natural contextual replies and naturally surfaced continuity. This is the only gameplay situation authorized for this pass. Keep changes local for review, with no commit or push. No new lore, runtime AI dependency, general planner or broad relationship/engine rewrite is authorized.
