# FREAK//CITY — first-night feedback

Play once, making the choices you would actually make. Use the settings as you wish. Please leave this form until after you finish or decide to stop. There are no right answers, and stopping early is useful feedback too. Do not read the other project documents beforehand.

1. What happened tonight? Describe it in your own words, without reopening the game.
2. Which people do you remember most clearly? What ordinary thing do you remember about each?
3. Did your opinion of anyone change? Describe the moment and why.
4. Describe a decision you found difficult. What did you want, and what made the alternatives costly?
5. Was there a moment of attraction, closeness, embarrassment or tension that worked for you? Was there one that felt forced or unwelcome? You may leave this blank.
6. Did anyone surprise you, disagree with you or seem occupied with their own life? Give an example, if one comes to mind.
7. What, if anything, seemed to return from an earlier conversation? How did that feel?
8. Where did you want to slow down? Where did you want the game to move on? Name a scene or describe it.
9. How did you use the phone? Were messages welcome, confusing, easy to miss, or too frequent?
10. Did you try to do anything the game would not let you do? Did it seem fair, unclear or broken?
11. Did the settings and choices give you the control you expected? You do not need to disclose your personal preferences.
12. Who would you seek out on another night, and what would you want to learn or do with them?
