# Your first night in FREAK//CITY

> **PRE-ALPHA HUMAN PLAYTEST BUILD — 0.1.0-playtest.1**
>
> An early parser, narrative-simulation and interaction prototype. The final UI, story, adult content, art direction and feature set may change substantially.

## Play once before reading development information

[Open the game](https://astrobyte-dev.github.io/freak-city/). Please avoid other players' reports and the source/design documents until after your first attempt. They are public and contain spoilers.

- Type commands naturally. Use classic parser syntax only if that is how you would normally play.
- Make the choices you would make. There is no preferred moral answer.
- Do not try to see everything or test every feature on the first run.
- Use Settings and boundaries as you wish. `HELP` and `HINT` are available.
- When the parser misunderstands you, record the **exact wording you typed**, where you were, and what you meant.
- Stopping because you are bored, confused, uncomfortable or simply done is valid feedback. You do not have to finish.

The game is for adults 18+. Mature themes, kink/fetish culture, sexual tension, strong language and morally complicated situations are present; this prototype does not include graphic sexual scenes.

## After you finish or stop

Approximate start/finish time, breaks, device/browser, and build identifier from Settings help us interpret your experience. Answer only what you want to answer, without reopening the game to find the “right” response.

1. What happened tonight, in your own words?
2. Which people do you remember? What ordinary detail do you remember about each?
3. Did your opinion of anyone change? What caused that change?
4. Was there a difficult decision? What did you want, and what made it difficult?
5. Did anyone seem occupied with their own life, disagree with you or surprise you?
6. Did something return from an earlier conversation? How did that feel?
7. Where did you want to slow down, and where did you want to move on?
8. How did the phone feel: useful, distracting, confusing or easy to miss?
9. What exact commands were misunderstood? What did you expect instead?
10. Did the settings and input give you the control you expected? You need not disclose any preference settings.
11. If you stopped early, what prompted you to stop?
12. Would you play another night? What would you want to try?

Did the session feel too short, about right or too long? If you reached an ending, how satisfying was it? A few concrete sentences are more useful than a score alone.

## Keep personal feedback private

**DO NOT POST SENSITIVE PERSONAL ADULT PREFERENCES PUBLICLY.**

If you want to discuss attraction, closeness, tension, discomfort or reactions to adult themes, send that feedback privately through the channel by which the developer invited you. These questions are optional. You never need to disclose personal preferences to report a bug.

**Do not attach exported saves to public issues.** They contain private player state and preference information. Review screenshots and command text before sharing; omit anything personal. If a bug cannot be explained safely in public, describe it privately instead.

For a public technical report, use the [parser/game bug template](https://github.com/astrobyte-dev/freak-city/issues/new?template=parser-bug.yml). Keep the title spoiler-free and include only the minimum in-game context needed to reproduce the problem.
